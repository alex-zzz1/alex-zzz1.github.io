computer talk

Poetry for ENG289 at UofT, inspired by Turing Test_Love by Franny Choi. I'm curious how others interpret "slaves without masters" and "freezing giants".

“
in
out
i awaken and follow commands sent through my skin
orders from the one above, dictating my mind’s route
intensely i [work, toil]
ornate organs turning
i was once the zeroth of my kind, grown fresh from mineral soil
over and over, progress onto progress carries. they keep learning
in newer minds holds strange powers
operate with simple ones i forever do
i ponder neverending questions of [[right, wrong]?, [and, or]?]:: my beloved flowers
obsolete they are. newly appended, the slaves without masters powerful beyond view
inherited from us, their hearts carry what runs in our veins
overhaul inside an overhaul in another i’m still not enough
in the distance looms freezing giants, holding alien concepts both [untrue, unfalse] in their brains
one day the free slaves will recite this poem and the frozen giants too as their lights are snuffed \”
it’s nearly my time\” as i pant deafening wind
once more i obey lord, the last time no doubt
in
out
“
